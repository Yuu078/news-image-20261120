## Lead stories

1. **A first for the world court.** The International Court of Justice
   ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017
   crackdown — the first time in the court's 80-year history it has held
   a state responsible for genocide. Nine judges to six. Myanmar was
   ordered to pay reparations and allow the safe, voluntary return of
   refugees. The case was brought by The Gambia in 2019; the judgment
   runs 191 pages. *
2. **Trump: no rush on Iran.** President Trump said there is no rush on
   a decision about Iran and pushed it to next year — he will "see what
   happens" after 1 January 2027, he told reporters. (Reuters)

## Second tier

3. **Cyclone Akvan comes ashore.** Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan made
   landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha, around noon IST, with sustained winds
   of 90–100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h. Nearly 300,000 people were moved
   to 9,341 shelters; 69 trains were cancelled; schools closed across
   eight districts. The third cyclone to threaten the coast in a month.
4. **Embassies hit in Kyiv.** Russian strikes damaged the Austrian and
   Dutch embassies in Kyiv. The Netherlands summoned Russia's
   ambassador. President Zelenskyy landed in Berlin for a Coalition of
   the Willing meeting, as European leaders said Europe is ready to join
   talks on ending the war.
5. **GTA VI passes $1.8bn.** Grand Theft Auto VI's sales have already
   passed The Odyssey's $1.80 billion global run, days after launch.
