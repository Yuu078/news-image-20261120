The Hague · Justice

Myanmar guilty of genocide, world court rules

The International Court of Justice ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 crackdown — the first time in the court’s 80-year history it has held a state responsible for genocide. Nine judges to six. Myanmar was ordered to pay reparations and allow refugees a safe, voluntary return home. The case was brought by The Gambia in 2019.

Odisha · Weather

Akvan comes ashore: 300,000 moved to safety

Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan made landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha, around noon, winds 90–100 km/h gusting to 110. Nearly 300,000 people were moved to 9,341 shelters; 69 trains cancelled. The third cyclone to threaten the coast in a month.
